How Lost2Found Works

A 5-step verified workflow designed to prevent false claims and protect privacy.

1. Report Item

Submit item details, date, campus location, and optional photos. Sensitive marks are shielded from public view.

2. Smart Matching

Our 6-factor algorithm matches lost items against found reports by category, campus zone, date, and description overlap.

3. Verify & Handover

Answer hidden ownership questions. Once staff approves your claim, collect your item using your secure verification code.
