About Lost2Found

Reimagining campus lost & found management into a trusted, centralized digital service

The Problem We Solve

In colleges, thousands of personal belongings like mobile phones, laptops, chargers, wallets, college ID cards, keys, earphones, and notebooks are misplaced every semester. Historically, students relied on scattered WhatsApp group messages, physical notice boards, or informal social media chatter.

Lost2Found provides one centralized, campus-authenticated platform that connects lost item reporters, item finders, and campus security officers with built-in fraud prevention.

Architectural Pillars

Robust MongoDB Backend

Production MongoDB Atlas data structures for reports, claims, audit logs, and categories.

Privacy Shielding

Confidential ownership identifiers are hidden from the public to prevent opportunistic false claims.

Secure Handover Codes

Unique verification codes required for physical item collection at the Security Office.

Nodemailer OTP Alerts

Automatic delivery of OTP codes and claim approval notifications directly to college inboxes.
