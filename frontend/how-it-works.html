Campus Standard Protocol

How Lost2Found Works

Understanding our 5-step campus recovery, smart matching, and privacy protection workflow

1

Report Lost or Found Item

Any verified student, faculty, or security personnel can submit an item report. Fill in the category, campus location (e.g. Computer Lab 3, Central Canteen), color, approximate date, and general public description. You can also upload photos.

2

Automatic Privacy Shield Active

To prevent fraud and fake claims, sensitive identifying details (such as the exact amount of cash inside a wallet, ID card numbers, laptop serial codes, or sticker specs) are stored privately and concealed from public view.

3

6-Factor Smart Matching Engine

When a report is submitted, our automated weighted engine evaluates all active campus findings. It scores Category match (25%), Location proximity (25%), Color (15%), Brand (10%), Date (15%), and Description keywords (10%) to highlight high-confidence matches.

4

Submit Claim & Ownership Proof

When you spot your item in the Found directory or via Smart Match, click "This Might Be Mine". Answer specific ownership verification questions (what was inside, unique scratches) and attach proof if available. Campus staff will review your answers.

5

Secure Handover Code Verification

Upon claim approval by security/staff, a unique verification code (e.g. L2F-8492) is generated and sent via email and in-app alert. Bring your student ID and present this code at the Security Office to safely collect your item.
